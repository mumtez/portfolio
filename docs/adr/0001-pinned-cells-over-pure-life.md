# Pinned Cells instead of pure Game of Life

Content drawn in cells is held alive by Pinned Cells that ignore B3/S23, rather than being built only from patterns that are stable under the real rules. Pure Life would limit text to chunky still-life glyphs and make anything beyond headings unreadable; pinning keeps text legible while every Free Cell still runs real Life and collides with the content. Strict Life is reserved for Set Pieces. Don't "fix" pinning back to pure rules without solving legibility first.
