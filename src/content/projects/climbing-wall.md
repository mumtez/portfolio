I love rock climbing and wanted to be able to train at home, so I built a freestanding climbing wall.

<!-- frame: wall -->

## The build

The wall is a wooden frame faced with plywood. It stands on its own A-frame, so it doesn't need a house wall to bolt to.

<!-- frame: frame -->

<!-- frame: aframe -->

<!-- frame: finished -->

## An adjustable angle

The wall is mounted on heavy-duty gate hinges. The A-frame can bolt into any of several mounting holes along the base, and moving it to a different hole changes the wall's angle.

<!-- frame: hinge -->

<!-- frame: steep -->

## Holds

It's set with holds I cut from wood alongside store-bought climbing holds.
