I captained FIRST Tech Challenge Team 14450 RoboRebels from 2022 to 2026. Over those seasons I designed, built and programmed three competition robots, with linear slide, arm and flywheel auto-targeting systems.

## Results

- Won the international 2025 Mexico Premier Event as the 1st seed (6-0)
- Two-time New Jersey State Championship finalist
- Won 2 Innovate Awards and 2 Control Awards

## Mentoring

We mentored FTC teams around the world, including teams in Australia, Morocco and Venezuela.
